Security & Architecture

OWASP PBKDF2-SHA256

Passwords are never stored in plaintext. They are salted with 128-bit random salts and derived using 600,000 PBKDF2 iterations completely offline.

Closed Shadow DOM

The lock screen runs inside a closed Shadow DOM container, isolating your password input from webpage scripts and Instagram styles.

Ephemeral Session Storage

Authentication state lives in chrome.storage.session and resets when the browser closes, when you lock your computer, or on timeout.

Extension Scope & Boundaries

Like any browser extension, protection does not prevent someone with local computer access from disabling extensions in chrome://extensions. Keep your OS account locked when away.
